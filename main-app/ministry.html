Visitor care & journey

Track actual visits, contact, membership interest and discipleship. Service headcounts are available separately under Attendance stats.

Visitor journey

First visits and returns use recorded visit dates, not database capture dates. Contact speed uses the first recorded successful contact after the first visit; historical missing dates are not guessed.

Fair follow-up assignment

Mark workers available, then distribute unassigned visitors to the available worker with the fewest unfinished assignments. Existing assignments stay in place; leaders can reassign from a profile.

Pastoral attention queue

Flag a visitor from their profile. Urgent flags appear here; contact a pastor directly when immediate help is needed.
