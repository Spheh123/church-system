STREAMS OF JOY JOHANNESBURG

Administrator training manual

Manage staff access, distribute visitor care, check activity, and keep the system ready for the team.

Access and help

Open Team Login on your phone or computer. Use your own email and the password supplied by the church administrator. There is no public staff signup. For a forgotten password, ask the administrator to generate another one. Never share an account.

Use Sign out when finished, especially on a shared device. Signing in, approximate location, device information, and activity are recorded. An internet connection is required. If a save fails, keep your work open and read the message before trying again.

Respect each visitor

Use contact information only for authorised church care. Follow the visitor's WhatsApp preference. Keep prayer requests confidential. Notes are shared with authorised leaders and the person assigned to follow up; they are not private personal notes. Record facts and agreed next steps, without unnecessary sensitive details.

1. Create staff access

  1. Open Dashboard → Admin Controls.
  2. Enter the staff member's name and correct email.
  3. Select their role: admin, pastor, or team. Give admin access only to people responsible for system administration.
  4. Select Create user. Wait for confirmation and the generated password.
  5. Give the person their login link, email and generated password privately. Do not place passwords in group chats or training manuals.
  6. Ask them to sign in and confirm their correct starting page.

2. Reset or remove access

In Access Overview, find the correct account and choose Generate password when a reset is needed. The previous password is replaced. Give the new password to that person privately. Use Disable access when someone leaves the role; use Restore access only when authorised. Disabling does not delete their historic notes. The system protects your own access and the last active admin from being disabled through these controls.

3. Receive and assign visitors

  1. After service, compare the ushers' capture count with the new people received.
  2. Open People and select a visitor.
  3. Under Follow-up Management, choose Assigned to, set a next follow-up date if needed, and save.
  4. Repeat until every visitor needing contact has an owner.
  5. Use Correct profile details → Save profile to fix a verified typo. Ask the usher for the original card if uncertain.

The dashboard's “New in the Last 48 Hours” uses capture time. The service date entered by an usher is recorded in Service Feedback. A visitor captured late may therefore appear as new today.

4. Monitor work and logins

Review Awaiting First Contact, Overdue Follow-ups, Prayer Priority, and Follow-Up Snapshot. Open a person's Activity History to inspect recorded views and changes.

Staff Login History shows recorded sign-ins, device/IP and approximate location when supplied, last activity, and estimated active duration. Location is not GPS and may reflect a mobile provider or VPN. A disconnected session is not proof of a deliberate logout. Active duration is an estimate from interaction checks, not a timesheet. Historical sessions before this feature was installed cannot be reconstructed.

System Activity shows recent recorded actions. Browser view logging depends on the client and connection; it is not a guarantee that every view under every failure condition is captured.

5. Reports

  1. Open Reports.
  2. Choose dates, status and assigned user as needed; inspect the preview.
  3. Select Export filtered CSV for a spreadsheet file.
  4. Send to Google Sheets requires the separate reporting integration to be configured. If it reports an error, use CSV and contact the system maintainer.

Date filters refer to capture time. Daily Google Sheets delivery only runs after its integration is configured and enabled. Keep exported files restricted to authorised recipients.

Weekly routine

Review disabled/stale accounts, unassigned visitors, overdue care, possible duplicate entries and failed exports. Check the intake with the ushers. Do not delete records or change database settings casually; request verified corrections from the system maintainer.

Practice with the trainer

Use a designated training account and visitor: create access, assign the visitor, verify the team can see only their assignment, confirm a saved note on your screen, export a filtered CSV, and inspect the recorded login. Do not use real visitor details for demonstrations.
