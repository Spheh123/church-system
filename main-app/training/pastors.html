STREAMS OF JOY JOHANNESBURG

Pastors’ training manual

See the care needs of the church, oversee follow-up, and support the team with clear next steps.

Access and help

Open Team Login on your phone or computer. Use your own email and the password supplied by the church administrator. There is no public staff signup. For a forgotten password, ask the administrator to generate another one. Never share an account.

Use Sign out when finished, especially on a shared device. Signing in, approximate location, device information, and activity are recorded. An internet connection is required. If a save fails, keep your work open and read the message before trying again.

Respect each visitor

Use contact information only for authorised church care. Follow the visitor's WhatsApp preference. Keep prayer requests confidential. Notes are shared with authorised leaders and the person assigned to follow up; they are not private personal notes. Record facts and agreed next steps, without unnecessary sensitive details.

1. Review the dashboard

After signing in, start with Prayer Priority, then Awaiting First Contact and Overdue Follow-ups. The new visitor panel covers records captured in the last 48 hours. Open People for the full directory; the dashboard only shows a short list in some panels.

2. Review a person

  1. Open their name to see Full Profile and Prayer Points.
  2. Read the notes and activity history before contacting the visitor.
  3. Record a concise pastoral next step in Notes → Add note. These are shared care notes, not a confidential counselling journal.
  4. If needed, correct verified profile details with Correct profile details.

3. Direct follow-up

Choose an owner under Assigned to, set a status and next follow-up date, enter an update summary, and select Save follow-up changes. The assigned team member can then work on that person. Confirm urgent assignments directly with the team; there is no automatic emergency notification.

StatusWhen to use it
PendingNo first contact attempt has been recorded.
ContactedYou reached the visitor and had a conversation.
Not reachableYou tried but could not reach the visitor. Add the attempt and next step.
Follow Up AgainAnother conversation is needed. Set a next follow-up date.
CompletedThe agreed follow-up is finished. Add an outcome note.
Voicemail / Not InterestedUse when accurate. Respect a request not to be contacted.

Some older records show Contacted (legacy). Do not change statuses just to improve the dashboard numbers.

4. Understand staff oversight

Access Overview lists staff roles and last activity. Staff Login History shows recorded sessions and approximate location/device information. Location is not an exact address; network routing and VPNs can change it. Active time is estimated, and a disconnected session may mean a closed tab or lost connection. Discuss unusual activity with the admin before drawing conclusions.

Staff account creation, generated password resets and access disabling are managed by the administrator in this version. Ask the admin when a team member needs access or a replacement password.

5. Review progress and reports

Use the Follow-Up Board for an overview of statuses, and Reports for date/status/owner filters. Export a filtered CSV when a leadership meeting needs it. Google Sheets export works only when the reporting integration has been configured. Captured At means database entry time; the service date supplied by the usher appears in Service Feedback.

Suggested weekly rhythm

  1. After Sunday service: confirm new visitors arrived and urgent care needs have an owner.
  2. Early in the week: check first contacts and requested return calls.
  3. Before the next service: review outstanding invitations, membership interest and overdue follow-ups.
  4. At the team review: discuss outcomes and obstacles, using notes and statuses together.

Practice with the trainer

Find a designated training visitor, review the prayer request, assign follow-up, add a note, and confirm the team member can see it. Review a filtered report and explain why estimated login duration is not an attendance record.
