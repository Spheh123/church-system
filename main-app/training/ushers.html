STREAMS OF JOY JOHANNESBURG

Ushers’ training manual

Welcome visitors, capture their information once, and pass the care journey to the follow-up team. No staff login is required.

1. Open the public form

Bookmark Open the public visitor form. You can also find this link on Team Login before signing in. The public form lets you submit information; it does not let you browse the church database.

2. Before capturing a visitor

  1. Explain that the church will use the information for visitor care and follow-up. Confirm the visitor agrees.
  2. Check that another usher has not already entered the same visitor from the same card. Choose one capture method per visitor; do not enter the same card into both the Google Form and this form.
  3. Have the visitor's completed card ready. Only type answers they actually provided. Leave optional unanswered fields blank.

3. Enter the information

  1. Check Service date. It starts with today's Johannesburg date. If capturing an earlier service, change it.
  2. Enter Full name accurately. This is required.
  3. Enter their email and cellphone if provided. Double-check every phone digit; a wrong number prevents follow-up.
  4. Enter the service attended, area, birthday and other supplied answers.
  5. Copy prayer points respectfully. Do not add guesses or personal opinions.
  6. Record the visitor's answer about WhatsApp and membership. Agreement to follow-up does not automatically mean agreement to a WhatsApp group.
  7. Tick the agreement checkbox only after the visitor has agreed.

4. Save and enter the next visitor

  1. Select Submit visitor form once.
  2. Wait while the button says Saving visitor…. Do not close the page.
  3. Look for the success message with the visitor's name and reference.
  4. The visitor fields clear; the service date and service name stay selected for the next entry. Check these before continuing.

If something goes wrong

If there is no success message, do not assume it saved. Keep the form open. If the result is uncertain, ask an admin to check the visitor's name before submitting again. If you entered a wrong number or duplicate, tell the admin the visitor's name and reference; do not submit a second form as a correction.

This form does not save an offline draft. Retain the original visitor card securely until the admin confirms capture, according to church procedure.

End-of-service handover

Tell the admin how many visitor cards were captured and which need checking. Hand urgent prayer or care needs directly to the responsible pastor; submitting the form does not send an emergency alert. Clear the device screen before handing it to someone else.

Practice with the trainer

Explain where the form link is, identify today's service date, and describe the success message. For a live practice submission, first ask the trainer to designate a clearly labelled training record so practice data is not mistaken for a real visitor.
