How Sunday visitors become cared-for people

  1. Ushers capture agreed visitor details through the public form.
  2. Admin or pastors review each visitor and assign an owner.
  3. Follow-up team contact assigned visitors and save outcomes, notes and next dates.
  4. Pastors and admin review prayer needs, progress, overdue care and reports.

Saved changes are shared with authorised users while connected. A public visitor submission does not grant access to staff pages.
