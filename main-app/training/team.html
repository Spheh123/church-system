STREAMS OF JOY JOHANNESBURG

Follow-up team training manual

Work through the people assigned to you, record each contact outcome, and keep the next action clear.

Access and help

Open Team Login on your phone or computer. Use your own email and the password supplied by the church administrator. There is no public staff signup. For a forgotten password, ask the administrator to generate another one. Never share an account.

Use Sign out when finished, especially on a shared device. Signing in, approximate location, device information, and activity are recorded. An internet connection is required. If a save fails, keep your work open and read the message before trying again.

Respect each visitor

Use contact information only for authorised church care. Follow the visitor's WhatsApp preference. Keep prayer requests confidential. Notes are shared with authorised leaders and the person assigned to follow up; they are not private personal notes. Record facts and agreed next steps, without unnecessary sensitive details.

1. Start your session

  1. Sign in. Your starting page is the Follow-Up Board.
  2. Open a person's card to see their profile and prayer points.
  3. You can also use People to find someone assigned to you. You cannot see everyone's visitors.
  4. If your list is empty, ask an admin or pastor to assign people to your account.

2. Prepare and contact

Read the existing notes before calling to avoid repeating a conversation. Check the contact details and prayer requests. Open WhatsApp opens the conversation in WhatsApp; it does not send a message automatically. Follow the visitor's preferences and the church's agreed contact times.

3. Record the outcome

  1. Choose the appropriate Follow-up status.
  2. For a return call, set Next follow-up date. Check your device's local time setting.
  3. Enter a short Update summary and select Save follow-up changes. Wait for confirmation.
  4. For a fuller conversation record, use Notes → Add note. Saving a note is separate from saving follow-up changes.
StatusWhen to use it
PendingNo first contact attempt has been recorded.
ContactedYou reached the visitor and had a conversation.
Not reachableYou tried but could not reach the visitor. Add the attempt and next step.
Follow Up AgainAnother conversation is needed. Set a next follow-up date.
CompletedThe agreed follow-up is finished. Add an outcome note.
Voicemail / Not InterestedUse when accurate. Respect a request not to be contacted.

Some older records show Contacted (legacy). Do not change statuses just to improve the dashboard numbers.

A useful note

“Spoke with visitor. They requested a call on Tuesday afternoon about membership. Follow-up arranged for Tuesday 15:00.” Avoid unverified claims and unnecessary personal information. Notes show the author and time.

4. Escalate and finish

Tell the pastor directly when a visitor needs urgent pastoral attention. Notes alone do not notify someone immediately. Ask a leader to correct profile details or change the assigned person. Before signing out, check that each completed call has its status, note and next step saved.

Real-time updates and troubleshooting

Saved changes become visible to authorised leaders and other authorised viewers while connected. If a record is reassigned, you may lose access. If your screen looks stale, check the connection and reload after saving. Do not overwrite another person's work without checking the latest notes.

Practice with the trainer

On a designated training record: open the profile, add a factual note, select Follow Up Again, set a next date, save, then reopen it to verify. Ask the trainer to confirm the change is visible on the leader's screen.
